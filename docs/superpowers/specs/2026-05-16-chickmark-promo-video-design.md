# ChickMark Promo Video Design

## Task

Create a funny promotional video concept for ChickMark, starting with a simple
actual video file rather than only a written script.

## Audience

The promo targets hatchery owners and managers who may decide whether to adopt
ChickMark. It should also be entertaining enough for social media and general
brand awareness.

## Approved Direction

The first video uses a blended direction:

- Tiny Manager: the ChickMark chick behaves like a cheerful but strict miniature
  hatchery supervisor.
- Boss Mode Dashboard: the product visuals feel polished, fast, and credible,
  especially around dashboard and BMK benchmark decisions.

The comedy comes from the contrast between the adorable mascot and its
impatience with messy audit numbers.

## Format

- Primary first deliverable: vertical 9:16 video for short-form social media.
- First cut length: 10-15 seconds.
- Future expanded cut: 25-35 seconds after real app footage is selected or
  captured.
- Language: English.
- Voiceover: none.
- Audio style: upbeat tech promo beat with a few funny sound-effect cues.
- End card: "Meet ChickMark."

## Product Focus

The first simple version should focus on dashboard and BMK benchmark value:

- BMK benchmarks become easier to read.
- Dashboard signals support cleaner hatchery decisions.
- The video should not try to tour the full app in the first cut.

Offline-first audits, station visits, and Govee capture may be used in a later
expanded version, but they are not required for the first video.

## Short Reel Storyboard

### 0-2s: Suspicious Numbers

Messy clipboard cards wobble into frame. A benchmark number, chart line, and
loose egg icon drift apart as if the audit data has started acting on its own.

On-screen caption:

> When the hatchery numbers start freelancing...

Motion and sound:

- Quick paper-slide motion.
- Tiny error chirp or notification sound.
- Slight wobble on the chart and numbers.

### 2-5s: Tiny Manager Enters

The ChickMark chick pops out from the checkmark, taps a tiny badge, and stamps
the chaotic items with animated blue checks.

Chick caption bubble:

> Absolutely not.

Motion and sound:

- Pop sound as the chick appears.
- Fast checkmark stamp sounds.
- A short "boss mode" sting.

### 5-10s: Boss Mode Dashboard

Dashboard-style cards snap into place with clean app-promo motion. Suggested
cards:

- BMK
- Trend
- Visit Summary
- Decision Ready

On-screen caption:

> BMK benchmarks. Clearer decisions. Fewer mystery spreadsheets.

Motion and sound:

- Cards slide, lock, and tick into alignment.
- Subtle blue glow or check animation as each card resolves.
- Beat stays energetic but not chaotic.

### 10-15s: Logo Payoff

The chick rides the checkmark across the screen, lands inside the egg outline,
and the final title appears.

End card:

> Meet ChickMark.

Motion and sound:

- Final checkmark sweep.
- Logo lockup.
- Short clean audio sting.

## Visual Style

The first version should be clean and bright:

- White or very light background.
- ChickMark blue and yellow as dominant brand colors.
- Minimal supporting colors for charts and dashboard cards.
- Rounded dashboard-card shapes and simple animated metric elements.
- No cluttered UI tour in the first cut.

The chick mascot may be animated from the existing ChickMark logo asset. The
first version can use simplified mascot motion, such as pop-in, bob, stamp,
ride-the-checkmark, and caption-bubble beats.

## Production Approach

Start with motion graphics rather than screen recordings:

- Use the existing ChickMark logo and mascot identity.
- Use animated text, simple icons, mock dashboard cards, and chart shapes.
- Keep captions short and readable on a phone.
- Leave room to later swap mock dashboard cards with real app screenshots or
  screen recordings.

The future expanded cut can reuse the same joke and add real app footage:

- A dashboard or BMK screen recording.
- A quick station-visit or audit-flow moment.
- Optional Govee temperature/RH capture beat if the longer cut needs a broader
  feature tour.

## Success Criteria

- The video is funny without making the product feel unserious.
- A hatchery manager can understand that ChickMark helps turn benchmark and
  dashboard data into clearer decisions.
- The short cut works without voiceover.
- The final frame clearly introduces the brand with "Meet ChickMark."
- The structure can later expand into a longer version with real app footage.

## Out of Scope For First Cut

- Full feature walkthrough.
- Voiceover recording.
- Real app footage capture.
- Horizontal or square exports.
- Arabic localization.
- Govee-focused storyline.
