Storyboard: Tiny Manager + Boss Mode

First vertical cut: 10-15 seconds. Later expanded cut: 25-35 seconds with app footage.

Short Reel Timeline
0-2s

Audit numbers are acting suspicious.

Messy clipboard cards wobble in. A benchmark number, chart line, and loose egg icon drift apart.

Caption: “When the hatchery numbers start freelancing...”

2-5s

The tiny manager enters.

The ChickMark chick pops out from the checkmark, taps a tiny badge, and stamps the chaos with animated blue checks.

Caption bubble: “Absolutely not.”

5-10s

Boss Mode Dashboard.

Slick dashboard cards snap into place: BMK, Trend, Visit Summary, Decision Ready. Cards move like a polished app promo.

Caption: “BMK benchmarks. Clearer decisions. Fewer mystery spreadsheets.”

10-15s

Logo payoff.

The chick rides the checkmark across the screen, lands inside the egg outline, and the final title appears.

End card: “Meet ChickMark.”

Production Notes

Look: Clean white background, ChickMark blue/yellow, energetic dashboard-card motion.

Sound: Upbeat tech beat, small pop/whoosh/checkmark sounds, one tiny “boss mode” sting.

First version: Motion graphics from logo, captions, simple chart/cards. No voiceover.

Later upgrade: Replace dashboard-card placeholders with real app footage and add a longer 25-35s cut.
